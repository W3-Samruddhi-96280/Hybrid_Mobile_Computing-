About C-DAC ACTS

C-DAC's Advanced Computing Training School, known as ACTS, provides professional training programmes in advanced computing and related technology areas.

These programmes are useful for students and graduates who want to build careers in software development, data science, artificial intelligence, cyber security, cloud computing and embedded systems.

Popular C-DAC Course Categories

Post Graduate Certificate Programmes

C-DAC offers PG Certificate Programmes in specialised areas such as advanced computing, artificial intelligence, cyber security, data science and embedded systems.

These programmes are designed to improve practical technical skills and prepare students for industry-oriented job roles.

Certificate Courses

C-DAC also conducts certificate courses such as CCST and CCACA for learners who want focused training in selected computing areas.

Collaborative and Special Programmes

ACTS also lists academic collaborative programmes, international collaborative programmes and special programmes for different learning requirements.

Admission Process

Admission to many C-DAC PG Certificate Programmes is done through C-DAC's Common Admission Test, also called C-CAT.

Candidates usually register online, appear for C-CAT and then participate in the counselling and seat allocation process based on their rank and preferences.
