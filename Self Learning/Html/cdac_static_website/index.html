Welcome to C-DAC

C-DAC is a premier Indian research and development organization working in advanced computing, electronics, software technologies, cyber security, health informatics, education and training.

Home

The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing, popularly known as C-DAC, works on national technology missions and develops solutions in information technology and electronics. Its activities include research, development, education, training, and deployment of technology solutions for different sectors.

Research & Development

C-DAC works in areas such as high performance computing, artificial intelligence, multilingual computing, cyber security and software technologies.

Education & Training

C-DAC offers advanced ICT skill development programmes through its Advanced Computing Training School.

Centres Across India

C-DAC has centres in cities such as Pune, Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Noida, Patna and Thiruvananthapuram.
