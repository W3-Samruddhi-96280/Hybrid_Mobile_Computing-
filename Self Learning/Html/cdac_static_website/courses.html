Courses

C-DAC's Advanced Computing Training School provides skill development programmes in advanced ICT areas. The programmes are designed for students and professionals who want to build careers in computing, software, electronics and related technologies.

Post Graduate Certificate Programmes

Advanced ICT skill development programmes of 1200 hours for engineering, science postgraduate and MCA candidates.

Certificate Course in Software Testing

A certificate-level course focused on software testing concepts, tools and practical skills.

Certificate Course in Advanced Computer Arts

A course designed for learners from non-engineering backgrounds interested in computer arts and creative technology.

Academic Collaborative Programmes

Certificate courses, faculty development programmes and workshops conducted with academic partners.

International Collaborative Programmes

ICT training collaborations and centres of excellence created under international initiatives.

Special Programmes

Capacity-building programmes sponsored by central and state ministries and other organizations.

Course categories are based on the official C-DAC ACTS courses page.
