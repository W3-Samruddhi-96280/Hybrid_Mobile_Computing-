About Us

C-DAC is a premier research and development organization of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. It carries out work in information technology, electronics and related areas. The organization was set up in 1988 with a major focus on building indigenous supercomputing capabilities.

Over time, C-DAC has expanded into multiple domains such as Indian language computing, health informatics, software technologies, cyber security, high performance computing and education. It supports national technological capability by creating products, services and solutions for government, industry and society.

Vision

To strengthen national capabilities in advanced computing and emerging information technologies.

Mission

To design, develop and deploy IT and electronics solutions for different sectors of the economy.

Values

Innovation, knowledge, public service, collaboration and excellence in technology development.
